# Final 3D models

Both models independently scored 9.0/10 after six-view review of their exported GLBs reimported into Blender.

- boy1/model.glb and boy1/model.blend: pale face and dark violet hair.
- boy2/model.glb and boy2/model.blend: gray face, cyan hair, bright yellow eyes and teeth.

Each folder contains front, side, rear and three-quarter previews, inspection.json, and REVIEW.md. The original references are in references/. The GLBs embed their 4K textures; Blender files include the review lighting and packed textures.

Tripo Studio credits: 110 spent of a 250 ceiling, 140 unused. Two direct image-to-3D generations at 55 credits each; final refinements were local.

Rear surfaces are inferred from the supplied front views. Minor surface imperfections are recorded in each REVIEW.md. Original downloads and intermediate attempts remain in the parent project's boy1/ and boy2/ folders.
